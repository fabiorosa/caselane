# CaseLane product brief

## One sentence

CaseLane gives small service teams one place to receive, triage, work, and
close client requests without losing context across inboxes and spreadsheets.

## Problem

Small service businesses often receive work through email, messaging apps,
forms, and meetings. Requests arrive without consistent fields, ownership, or
status. Team members manually copy information between tools, clients ask for
updates, and managers cannot see what is blocked or overdue.

## Primary users

### Owner or administrator

Creates the workspace, invites the team, manages clients, controls membership,
and monitors the operation.

### Team member

Triages requests, owns work, communicates with clients, and moves cases through
the workflow.

### Client contact

Submits requests, adds context and files, and follows progress without seeing
internal notes or other clients.

## Core job

When a client asks for work, the team needs to turn an unstructured request into
an owned, traceable case and keep both sides aligned until it is resolved.

## Product principles

1. Every request has an owner or is visibly unassigned.
2. Client communication and internal discussion are clearly separated.
3. Status always explains what is happening and who must act next.
4. The activity log is trustworthy and append-only from the user's perspective.
5. The client portal is intentionally simpler than the internal workspace.
6. AI may suggest; a person decides and remains accountable.

## Success for the portfolio release

A reviewer can use seeded demo accounts to experience the complete lifecycle:

1. submit a request as a client;
2. triage it as a team member;
3. assign it, communicate, and change its status;
4. resolve it and inspect the full activity history;
5. verify that roles and organizations cannot access each other's data.

No commercial adoption or fictional performance metric is required.
